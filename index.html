About Ashoka International

Ashoka International is a trusted overseas recruitment agency based in Nizamabad, Telangana. With over 20 years of experience, we have helped thousands of candidates build successful careers abroad. We specialize in employment visas, overseas recruitment, documentation, and complete travel assistance for Gulf Countries, Russia, and Turkey.
